3D UAV Path Planning: Simulation to Real-World Control

Research & Systems Engineering Flagship

Primary Research Contribution: A comparative investigation of reinforcement-learning algorithms (PPO, DDPG, DQN) and environmental input representations for autonomous 3D UAV path planning, successfully integrated into a real-time physical UAV control framework.

1. Simulation & Research Environment

Before touching hardware, I built a custom 3D UAV simulation environment from scratch. This was not a pre-packaged RL tutorial; the environment was specifically engineered to evaluate different approaches to autonomous 3D path planning.

  • Custom 3D Workspace: Programmed UAV kinematics, configurable obstacles, position/orientation tracking, and precise collision/distance calculations.
  • Algorithm Comparison: Implemented and trained agents using PPO, DDPG, and DQN, benchmarking their navigation performance against classical Artificial Potential Field (APF) approaches.
  • Investigation of Agent Inputs: A major focus of the research was analyzing how different environmental perceptions affected path-planning success. I experimented with and compared:
    • Depth-camera-based observations.
    • APF-based sensor representations.
    • Direct distance and directional vectors to the closest obstacles.

2. Real-Time Experimental Platform

The transition from simulation to real-world deployment presented significant software and hardware integration challenges. The laboratory's VICON motion-capture system was strictly Windows-based, while the UAV control stack required a Linux environment. I engineered a real-time bridging architecture to close the control loop.

3. Physical UAV & Safety Systems

Running untested RL policies on expensive physical hardware is inherently risky. Safety was not an afterthought; it was deeply incorporated into the real-time control framework.

  • Continuous Monitoring: The Python application continuously monitored the UAV state against the defined physical boundaries of the VICON cage.
  • Pre-emptive Triggering: Programmed safety logic capable of calculating impending collisions and overriding the RL policy commands.
  • Emergency Landings: Developed both manual fail-safes and an automated safety landing protocol that safely brought the Parrot Anafi to the ground before a crash could occur.

    Project 2: Inverted Pendulum with Pole Placement Control

    Objective: Design and simulate a closed-loop control system to stabilize a highly unstable nonlinear dynamic system.

    Engineering Process

    • State-Space Modeling: Derived the non-linear dynamics of the inverted pendulum and linearized the system to create a state-space representation.
    • Controller Design: Designed a state-feedback controller using the Pole Placement technique to achieve desired closed-loop stability and transient response.
    • Closed-Loop Simulation: Simulated the controlled system to verify that the pendulum remains inverted despite initial disturbances, visualizing the stabilized response.
